## Current conclusion

The earlier critique used too broad a comparison standard. Problems that capitalism also handles imperfectly are not automatically objections to this model. The remaining work concerns enforcement of citizens' decisions, periodic review of resource use, spatial allocation of housing, and the legal status of personal buildings on common land. The possibility of changing basic rules is an explicit political risk.

## Publication workflow

Use the current master post, not the archived adaptations. Ask for causal mechanisms, historical counterexamples, quantitative estimates, agent-based models, or small experiments. A useful objection should show why the proposed mechanism creates a failure that transparency, competition, bonuses, or replaceable management cannot address.
