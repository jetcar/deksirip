# Canonical short formulation of the concept

Use the same formulation in every publication so that critics are discussing one object. Adapt only the title and introduction, not the model itself.

Assume a society with high productivity and automation in which basic needs are provided unconditionally. The means of production are socially owned and cannot be privately held. There is no central planning body.

Every citizen receives a recurring individual share of the social product. The unspent part accumulates in a personal account. Accumulation does not grant ownership of factories or land, but the citizen directs their share.

Any person or group may propose a project with a stated cost and a resource-use calendar. A project is funded if enough citizens direct their accumulated shares to it. A citizen may also join with labour. Ordinary production follows where people direct resources and labour. Existing enterprises are run by workers through a chosen organizer or management.

Long-term projects — research, medicines, energy, and infrastructure — may receive staged budgets reviewed as new information arrives. Strategic reserves are set by citizens; experts and AI provide information but do not decide. Competing requests for scarce physical resources are shown as explicit alternatives. Every decision is traceable: citizen decision → rule → data → calculation → allocation. Rules change only by citizen decision. The army is professional and voluntary, may act without a vote under direct attack, and has no independent economic base.

## Request for criticism

I am not looking for validation. I want concrete mechanisms in the form: X happens → people or organizations get an incentive to do Y → Z results → the system loses stability. Historical counterexamples and objections that cannot be fixed by parameter tuning are especially valuable.
