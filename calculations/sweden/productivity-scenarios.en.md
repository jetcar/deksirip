# Shorter working week and productivity growth

## Two different variables

Distinguish:

1. **necessary person-hours** — the labour required by the basic system;
2. **number of participating people** — how many people share those hours.

If necessary labour is 2.215 million FTE, moving from 40 to 20 hours does not reduce it. It increases the number of participants from 2.215 to 4.43 million.

To reduce both hours and participants, automation or organizational simplification must reduce `total_required_hours` itself.

| Area | Reduction potential | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Checkout and retail routine | high | returns, exceptions, restocking |
| Digital education | high | practice, special needs, socialization |
| Administrative accounting | high | audit and responsibility |
| Industrial flow | medium/high | repair, setup, emergencies |
| Energy system | medium | reliability and physical failures |
| Construction and repair | medium | unpredictable environments |
| Medicine | medium | complex diagnosis and emergency care |
| Physical care | low/medium | the person receiving care, not only the task |

First automate a concrete task, then measure saved hours and verify quality. Do not subtract a percentage from an entire sector in advance.
