# Assumptions and Sweden control scenarios

## Scenario S0: present-day Sweden as a control

S0 does not claim that current consumption is optimal or necessary. It checks the arithmetic against an existing economy and provides an upper reference point for labour requirements.

## Scenario S1: decent basic standard

S1 is defined separately through physical standards:

- sufficient food energy and protein, without reproducing excessive current consumption;
- housing area, heating, and repair;
- access to basic and emergency medicine and medicines;
- minimum public and freight transport;
- basic internet and emergency communications;
- basic schooling and training of necessary specialists;
- predefined capacity reserves.

S1 is not filled with numbers yet, so a value judgement is not disguised as a technical fact.

## Scenario S2: resilient standard

S2 adds replacement of worn capital, equipment and raw-material reserves, training of new specialists, research and technological capacity, and export labour required for imports unavailable in Sweden.

## Scenario S3: human minimum

S3 asks how much human labour remains after removing jobs that exist mainly because of economic coercion, poor organization, or outdated technology. The unit of analysis is the task, not the job title.

A cashier may disappear as a permanent position while restocking, error control, returns, and customer assistance remain. Cleaning may be automated while sanitation, equipment maintenance, and epidemiological control remain. Administrative routine may disappear while auditing rules and investigating exceptions remain. Production may become nearly unmanned while design, setup, repair, and emergency work still require people.

S3 includes only tasks for which the result is necessary, organizational simplification is insufficient, and no reliable automated replacement exists at the chosen horizon. It should have two variants:

- **S3a — mature automation:** existing technologies that can be scaled in Sweden;
- **S3b — high automation:** technically plausible systems that are not yet widespread.

The result is person-hours of irreplaceable human work, not the percentage of people holding a particular occupation. The minimum is distributed among willing workers through a shorter week and bonuses for unpleasant or dangerous tasks. Video lessons, automated testing, and personal AI reduce routine education work; practice, special needs, safety, socialization, and difficult cases remain.

## Interpretation rule

The share of working people is reported using at least two denominators: total population and working-age population. The average working week must also be shown. Equal person-hours can mean fewer workers at 40 hours or more workers at 20 hours.
