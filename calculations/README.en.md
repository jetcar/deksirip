# Calculations

This folder contains source data, tables, formulas, and results for the quantitative check of the model.

## Structure

- [sweden/](sweden/) — estimate of the labour required for a basic standard of living in Sweden.

## Calculation rules

- source data are separated from assumptions;
- physical quantities are not mixed with monetary quantities;
- all results are calculated for at least three working-time scenarios;
- every estimate includes a source, date, and uncertainty range;
- missing data are marked `TBD` rather than replaced with guesses.
