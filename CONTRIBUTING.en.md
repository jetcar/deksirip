# How to participate

The project is intended to be a fair test of the model, not to confirm a pre-selected conclusion.

A good objection contains:

- specific assumption of the model;
- causal chain `X → stimulus → behavior → consequence`;
- an indication of whether the problem is new to the model or common to existing systems;
- historical example, data, calculation or proposal for an experiment.

Phrases such as “people are greedy,” “this has already been tried,” or “capitalism is not perfect either” are not sufficient without a mechanism and a comparison.

When changing the model, indicate which underlying assumption has changed and what consequences this has for the remaining sections.
